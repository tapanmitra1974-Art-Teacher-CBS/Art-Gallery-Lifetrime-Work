Tapan Mitra

Born in 1947 in Kadamtala, Howrah, Tapan Mitra is a prolific and veteran Indian painter and printmaker. He is widely recognised as an early pioneer of silkscreen printmaking (serigraphy) in Bengal, maintaining a rigorous personal studio practice throughout his distinguished teaching career.

His work is deeply introspective, often using everyday objects and familiar forms to explore identity, silence, memory, and human presence through layered, restrained compositions.

For 34 years, Mitra served as the prominent visual arts teacher at Calcutta Boys' School (CBS), introducing generations of young students in Kolkata to the pleasures and intricacies of art until his retirement.

Career & Education

  • 1947 Born in Kadamtala, Howrah.
  • 1972 Completed Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Painting from Kala Bhavana, Visva-Bharati University, Santiniketan.
  • 1973 Specialised in Serigraphy under Professor Biswarup Bose (son of legendary artist Nandalal Bose).
  • 1974 Joined the faculty of Calcutta Boys' School, shaping the school's creative curriculum for over three decades.
  • Exhibitions Major solo shows in Kolkata, Chennai, and Dhaka. Participated in international group shows spanning the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Southeast Asia.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. Who we are

This website is the studio gallery of Tapan Mitra (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact info@tapanmitra.art.

2. What data we collect

  • Contact form data — your name, email address, subject and message, used solely to answer your enquiry.
  • Artwork data — titles, descriptions, dates, media, dimensions, prices, keywords and images you add to the gallery. These are stored securely in Google Firebase Cloud Storage and Firestore.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type) if this site is hosted on a web server.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use the minimum necessary to run the site:

  • Strictly necessary — cookie-consent and theme remember your preferences in your browser.
  • Analytics (optional) — if enabled, anonymous visit statistics help us understand which works are most viewed. These are only set with your consent.

4. How we use your data

  • To reply to enquiries and arrange sales or exhibitions.
  • To keep a record of artwork metadata for the catalogue.
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations.

We never sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

5. Legal basis

We process enquiry data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to you (Article 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR). Cookie preferences are processed on the basis of your consent.

6. How long we keep data

Contact enquiries are kept for up to 24 months. Artwork data remains in the cloud until deleted by an administrator.

7. Your rights

  • Access, correct or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Object to processing or request restriction.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@tapanmitra.art.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
